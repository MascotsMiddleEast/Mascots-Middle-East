🛡️ Guarantee & Terms

Before You Order

What we promise, and what to know before placing your order.

🛡️

Our Guarantee

If your mascot arrives damaged, we repair or replace it. Just send us photos within 3 days of delivery — that's all we need.

🕐

Before production starts

Changed your mind? Tell us as soon as possible. Your deposit is non-refundable — it covers the design work and the slot we reserve for you — but anything you've paid beyond the deposit comes back to you in full.

🔒

Once you approve the design

Production is locked. Our factory builds to your exact approved specifications and can't change anything mid-build, so the order can't be cancelled or refunded after this point.

📏

Sizing

Your mascot is built to the size you choose from our chart, so fit isn't grounds for return. Check the Sizes guide before you confirm.

🚚

Delivery times

Catalogue mascots 15–20 days, custom mascots 30–45 days. Customs and courier delays are outside our control, but we'll keep you updated throughout.

Mascot rentals are covered by a separate rental agreement.
